Osaka Food Guide 🍜

The Culinary Capital of Japan

Famous Foods of Osaka

Takoyaki

Osaka's iconic street food. Crispy on the outside, soft inside, filled with octopus and topped with sweet sauce, mayonnaise and bonito flakes.

Okonomiyaki

A savory Japanese pancake packed with cabbage, meat, seafood and delicious toppings. A must-try Osaka specialty.

Kushikatsu

Deep-fried skewers of meat, seafood and vegetables. One of Osaka's most famous comfort foods.
