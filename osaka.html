Osaka Food Guide 🍜

Famous Foods of Osaka

Takoyaki

Crispy octopus-filled balls served with sweet sauce, mayonnaise and bonito flakes.

Okonomiyaki

A savory Japanese pancake packed with vegetables, meat and delicious toppings.

Kushikatsu

Deep-fried skewers of meat and vegetables, one of Osaka's most famous street foods.
